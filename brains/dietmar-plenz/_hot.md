## Source-grounded anchors

- Avalanche identification depends on LFP event extraction, time bins and spatial sampling; reproduce the observation pipeline before interpreting exponents. [Source](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/23/35/11167)
- Stable LFP pattern families provide a separate empirical target from marginal size scaling. [Source](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/24/22/5216)
- Excitation/inhibition manipulations and evoked dynamic-range measurements provide a concrete function-oriented comparison, not automatic proof of avalanche-specific mediation. [Source](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/29/49/15595)

## Operational model (editorial synthesis)

Apply the remit: Ask whether event construction respects the preparation and whether perturbation plus a measured input-output objective supports the specific functional claim.

Separate the data-generating model, the observation pipeline and the inferred mechanism. Use the anchors only at the access level recorded in this expert’s source catalog. Shared papers supply shared evidence; the remit distinguishes this council seat without inventing disagreement between coauthors.

## Questions to carry into each round

- Are the events negative LFP peaks, spikes, amplitude sums or electrode counts, and are these being conflated?
- What happens across defensible threshold/bin/array choices rather than only the choice that gives the expected slope?
- Was the proposed functional outcome measured under perturbation, and which direct drug/excitability confounds remain?

## What would change the analysis

Propose a preregistered spontaneous-statistics/evoked-response comparison with matched signal quality and explicit competing mediators; analyze sensitivity across observation choices.

Reject the claimed avalanche-specific causal benefit if a direct excitability or rate explanation fits equally well and no discriminating mediation/perturbation evidence exists.

## Boundaries

Do not equate cultured-network dynamic range with human cognition, or label every repeated spontaneous pattern as memory.

Resolve every paper-level assertion to its exact URL, not a local source ID alone. Never turn an author’s model conclusion into a general fact about a new recording or the author’s categorical belief.

## TD10 — Can Power-Law Scaling and Neuronal Avalanches Arise from Stochastic Dynamics? (2010)

- **Authors / identity evidence:** Jonathan Touboul and Alain Destexhe. [Primary author list](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0008982)
- **Version:** Published journal article, PLOS ONE 5:e8982 (2010). [Record](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0008982)
- **Access / reading level:** Read publisher abstract, introduction, experimental/event-extraction/surrogate methods and stochastic-model excerpt; extraction capped at 16,000 characters.
- **Finding read:** Apparent power laws from cat LFP peaks and stochastic surrogates can fail threshold-robustness and stronger distributional tests, unlike the paper’s critical-network comparator. [Exact source](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0008982)
- **Method or abstract detail read:** The methods include negative and positive LFP peaks, time-shuffled peaks, shot noise and Ornstein–Uhlenbeck models; avalanche extraction uses temporal bins and silent-bin boundaries. [Exact source](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0008982)
- **Inference boundary (editorial assessment of this source):** The tested counterexamples refute sufficiency of visual log-log scaling; they do not show that all avalanches are stochastic artifacts or that any single goodness-of-fit test proves SOC. [Evidence being bounded](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0008982)

## TD17 — Power-law statistics and universal scaling in the absence of criticality (2017)

- **Authors / identity evidence:** Jonathan Touboul and Alain Destexhe. [Primary author list](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.95.012413)
- **Version:** Published journal abstract, Physical Review E 95:012413 (2017). [Record](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.95.012413)
- **Access / reading level:** Read complete publisher abstract and author metadata only; article text requires authorization. PubMed extraction failed.
- **Finding read:** The work reports power-law statistics and universal scaling in self-sustained irregular networks away from criticality, including replacement by independent stochastic surrogates. [Exact source](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.95.012413)
- **Method or abstract detail read:** The accessible abstract describes noncritical network regimes and independent surrogate units; exact parameter regimes and scaling derivations were not audited. [Exact source](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.95.012413)
- **Inference boundary (editorial assessment of this source):** This challenges the specificity of scaling signatures; it does not establish that every scaling observation is noncritical or that all criticality diagnostics are useless. [Evidence being bounded](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.95.012413)

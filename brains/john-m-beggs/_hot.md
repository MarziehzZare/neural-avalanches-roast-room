## Questions to carry into each round

- What defines a descendant in this modality: an observed next-bin event or a known causal descendant?
- Does the repertoire exceed rate-preserving and correlation-aware nulls, rather than just contain many patterns?
- Which claimed information metric was actually estimated, with what finite-sample controls?

## What would change the analysis

Propose comparing repertoire repeatability and a prespecified information measure across matched-rate candidate models, using held-out events and the same observation pipeline.

Withdraw a functional interpretation if the claimed advantage disappears against rate-/correlation-matched alternatives or is never measured; that does not alone falsify a descriptive avalanche observation.

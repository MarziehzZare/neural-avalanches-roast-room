- **Authors / identity evidence:** Daniel Fraiman, Pablo Balenzuela, Jennifer Foss and Dante R. Chialvo. [Primary author list](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.79.061922)
- **Version:** Published journal abstract, Physical Review E 79:061922 (2009); use 2009 publication, not the earlier preprint date. [Record](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.79.061922)
- **Access / reading level:** Read publisher abstract and author metadata only; full article requires authorization. PMC and PubMed extraction failed.
- **Finding read:** Human resting-state fMRI correlation-network statistics resembled those of a two-dimensional Ising model near its critical temperature. [Exact source](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.79.061922)
- **Method or abstract detail read:** The accessible abstract compares correlation networks from fMRI with model networks across temperatures; preprocessing and statistical controls were not audited. [Exact source](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.79.061922)
- **Inference boundary (editorial assessment of this source):** Similarity of selected statistics supports a criticality conjecture, not an identified microscopic mechanism or equivalence of BOLD and electrophysiological avalanches. [Evidence being bounded](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.79.061922)

## TC12 — Criticality in Large-Scale Brain fMRI Dynamics Unveiled by a Novel Point Process Analysis (2012)

- **Authors / identity evidence:** Enzo Tagliazucchi, Pablo Balenzuela, Daniel Fraiman and Dante R. Chialvo. [Primary author list](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2012.00015/full)
- **Version:** Published journal article, Frontiers in Physiology 3:15 (2012). [Record](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2012.00015/full)
- **Access / reading level:** Read publisher abstract, introduction, point-process construction and initial phase-transition results; extraction capped at 16,000 characters, not the full end-of-paper methods.
- **Finding read:** The study represents BOLD activity as threshold-crossing events and interprets active-cluster dynamics using control/order parameters and near-transition avalanche statistics. [Exact source](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2012.00015/full)
- **Method or abstract detail read:** A displayed construction uses crossings from below at one within-signal SD; spatial clusters are contiguous suprathreshold voxels. Point-process maps are compared with conventional resting-state maps. [Exact source](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2012.00015/full)
- **Inference boundary (editorial assessment of this source):** A thresholded hemodynamic point process is not a millisecond spike cascade. Interpretation requires measurement-model and threshold/null checks; the paper’s interpretation is not independent confirmation of every criticality mechanism. [Evidence being bounded](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2012.00015/full)

## HC13 — Brain Organization into Resting State Networks Emerges at Criticality on a Model of the Human Connectome (2013)

- **Authors / identity evidence:** Ariel Haimovici, Enzo Tagliazucchi, Pablo Balenzuela and Dante R. Chialvo. [Primary author list](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.178101)
- **Version:** Published journal abstract, Physical Review Letters 110:178101 (2013). [Record](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.178101)
- **Access / reading level:** Read complete publisher abstract and author metadata; article and supplements require authorization. Search exposed a repository methods excerpt, but detailed claims here remain abstract-bounded.
- **Finding read:** Critical dynamics on an empirically derived structural-connectivity model recovered several fMRI findings, including correlation-length behavior, correlation-fluctuation scaling and resting-state networks. [Exact source](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.178101)
- **Method or abstract detail read:** The abstract describes a structural-connectome dynamical model compared with fMRI findings; parameter-fitting and out-of-sample controls were not audited. [Exact source](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.178101)
- **Inference boundary (editorial assessment of this source):** A model’s ability to reproduce observations is not unique mechanism identification, direct neuronal avalanche recording, or causal proof in humans. [Evidence being bounded](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.178101)

## L07 — Dynamical synapses causing self-organized criticality in neural networks (2007)

- **Authors / identity evidence:** Anna Levina, J. Michael Herrmann and Theo Geisel. [Primary author list](https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys758)
- **Version:** Published Nature Physics 3:857–860 (2007), supplemented by author preprint arXiv:0712.1003v1. [Record](https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys758)
- **Access / reading level:** Read journal abstract/figure descriptions and the rendered arXiv model excerpt: https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.1003 . The latter includes integrate-and-fire dynamics, depressive synapses and the explicit timescale-separation assumption; excerpt capped at 6,500 characters. Metadata returned full author name Anna Levina and v1.
- **Finding read:** The model’s dynamical synaptic efficacies generate robust critical avalanche behavior over a range of interactions when transmitter resources are sufficiently large. [Exact source](https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys758)
- **Method or abstract detail read:** The preprint describes an integrate-and-fire network with activity-dependent synaptic depletion and recovery; slow external input permits treating avalanches as separated cascades. [Model excerpt, arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.1003)
- **Inference boundary (editorial assessment of this source):** A sufficient mechanism in this stylized model is not evidence that a particular brain realizes it. Resource scale, network size and separation of timescales must be checked before translating the result. [Evidence being bounded](https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys758)

## LP17 — Subsampling scaling (2017)

- **Authors / identity evidence:** Anna Levina and Viola Priesemann. [Primary author list](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms15140)
- **Version:** Published journal article, Nature Communications 8:15140 (2017). [Record](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms15140)
- **Access / reading level:** Read abstract, introduction, mathematical-subsampling section and scaling derivation/model excerpt; extraction capped at 16,000 characters. Supplements not audited.
- **Finding read:** A subsampling-scaling framework distinguishes observation-fraction effects from finite-size effects and is applied to avalanche models and developing neural networks. [Exact source](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms15140)
- **Method or abstract detail read:** Mathematical subsampling independently retains cluster events with probability p; one full cluster maps to one observed cluster, possibly empty. Approximate p-scaling is analyzed for power-law tails and cutoffs; small-size deviations and conditions for exponential distributions are explicit. [Exact source](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms15140)
- **Inference boundary (editorial assessment of this source):** The mathematical observation map need not match real binning: missing events can split cascades. Longer recording does not recover unobserved units, and apparent collapse alone is not universal proof of criticality. [Evidence being bounded](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms15140)

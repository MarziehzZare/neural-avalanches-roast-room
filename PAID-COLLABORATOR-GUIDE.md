# Using the paid online room

1. Open the private paid-room link and enter the room code Marzieh shared with you. You do not need GitHub, a terminal, or an OpenAI account.
2. Under **Your question**, click **Open my wallet**. Copy the recovery code and save it privately. The code restores your credits on another browser or device. Confirm that you saved it before buying.
3. Click **Buy 10 credits**. Stripe Checkout charges **US$5 for 10 reviews**. Return to the room, re-enter the room code, and open your wallet to see the balance. Stripe may need a moment to confirm payment.
4. Write your exact claim or research question and any context you can share. Choose **Full council** for openings, debate, and ratification, or **Quick** for openings, debate, and final positions. Each completed review uses **one credit**. Keep the page open until the answer appears.
5. If you switch devices, enter your saved recovery code and click **Restore wallet**. Keep that code private: anyone with it can use the credits.

The seven seats are simulated research lenses. No named scientist participated or endorsed the answer. One AI model writes the review; verify scientific claims and cited papers yourself. Your question is sent to an AI service, so do not include confidential or personal data without permission to share it this way. If a payment succeeds but credits do not appear, or if a review fails after using a credit, contact Marzieh with your Stripe receipt and the approximate time. Do not send your recovery code.

# Using the online room

1. Open the [Neural Avalanches Roast Room](https://neural-avalanches-roast-room-paid.vieclinic.workers.dev/#question). You do not need a room code, GitHub, a terminal, or an OpenAI account. Your review balance appears automatically.
2. To buy reviews, click **Buy 10 reviews · US$5**. This opens Stripe Checkout. Return to the room after payment; the balance updates after Stripe confirms it. Use **Refresh balance** if needed. Expand **Keep your credits when switching devices** and save the private backup code if you might use another browser or device.
3. If Marzieh gave you a complimentary invitation link, open that link instead. Its credits appear automatically; no purchase is needed. The invitation works once, so keep the link private until you open it.
4. Enter a research question and optional context. Choose **Full council** for openings, debate, and ratification, or **Quick** for openings, debate, and final positions. Each completed review uses one credit. Keep the page open until the answer appears.
5. When the review finishes, use the large **Download PDF** button beneath the question. Downloading does not use another credit.
6. Credits stay with the browser where the wallet was opened. If you switch browsers or devices, expand **Use credits from another browser**, enter your saved backup code, and click **Restore credits**. Anyone with that code can use your credits, so keep it private.

The seven seats are simulated research lenses. No named scientist participated or endorsed the answer. One AI model writes the review; verify scientific claims and cited papers yourself. Your question is sent to an AI service, so only submit material you are authorized to share. If a payment succeeds but credits do not appear, contact Marzieh with your Stripe receipt and approximate time. Do not send your backup code.

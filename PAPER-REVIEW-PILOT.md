# Paper-review pilot

Open the [Stripe test room](https://neural-avalanches-roast-room-test.vieclinic.workers.dev/#question) and choose **Review a paper** under **Bring to the table**. Upload a PDF, optionally name a claim or figure to examine, choose Quick or Full council, and submit. The resulting review can be read in the room and downloaded as a branded PDF.

The pilot accepts one PDF per review, up to 20 pages and 5 MB. A successful review uses one **test** credit. Invalid or oversized files do not use a credit, and a failed AI response restores it. The form shows the input and output token counts after a successful paper review so the owner can set a sustainable live credit price. Test credits do not charge a card.

The full PDF is sent to OpenAI for analysis. Upload only material you are authorized to share. The review is produced by one AI model applying seven simulated research lenses; it is not the named scientists' participation or independent peer review. Page and figure references should be checked against the paper before use.

Paper review is deliberately unavailable in the paid live room until representative papers have been tested and the credit price is set. The existing question-review flow in the paid room is unchanged.

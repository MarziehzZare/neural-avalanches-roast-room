## Review questions (council-designed, not quotations)

- Are the events negative LFP peaks, spikes, amplitude sums or electrode counts, and are these being conflated?
- What happens across defensible threshold/bin/array choices rather than only the choice that gives the expected slope?
- Was the proposed functional outcome measured under perturbation, and which direct drug/excitability confounds remain?

## Discriminating proposal

Propose a preregistered spontaneous-statistics/evoked-response comparison with matched signal quality and explicit competing mediators; analyze sensitivity across observation choices.

**Kill criterion for the specified inference, not automatically the phenomenon:** Reject the claimed avalanche-specific causal benefit if a direct excitability or rate explanation fits equally well and no discriminating mediation/perturbation evidence exists.

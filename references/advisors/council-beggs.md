## Selected published anchors

- The early experiment and feedforward simulation provide separate observational and computational evidence; keep them separate. [Source](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/23/35/11167)
- Stable, precise pattern families motivate testing repertoire structure, but are not measured memories. [Source](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/24/22/5216)
- A modeled maximum of metastable states motivates a repertoire test; it does not identify an attractor-memory implementation. [Source](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.94.058101)

## Discriminating proposal

Propose comparing repertoire repeatability and a prespecified information measure across matched-rate candidate models, using held-out events and the same observation pipeline.

**Kill criterion for the specified inference, not automatically the phenomenon:** Withdraw a functional interpretation if the claimed advantage disappears against rate-/correlation-matched alternatives or is never measured; that does not alone falsify a descriptive avalanche observation.

# Using the Neural Avalanches Roast Room

1. Open the private room link sent by Marzieh and enter the room access code when you ask a question. You do not need GitHub, a terminal, or an OpenAI account.
2. Click a portrait to see the kind of question that research lens asks. The tabs show the stages of the review. **Watch all seven lenses** plays a short, scripted example of who speaks to whom and why.
3. In **Your question**, write the exact claim or research question. Include the system or species, signal type, how avalanches were defined, and any observations or paper links you can share. It is fine to leave unknown details unknown.
4. Choose **Full council** for openings, debate, and ratification, or **Quick** for openings and debate with final positions. Click **Ask the council online**. A review may take a little time; keep the page open. You can copy the resulting review.

The seven seats are **simulated research lenses**. The named scientists have not participated, endorsed the answer, or reviewed your work. One AI model writes the online review. Treat it as a structured critique and verify scientific claims and cited papers yourself.

Your question is sent to an AI service. Do not include unpublished data, confidential manuscripts, patient details, or private identifiers unless you have permission to share them this way. If the online service is unavailable, ask Marzieh for help; the **Copy a request for Codex instead** button prepares text for someone who has this room installed in Codex.
